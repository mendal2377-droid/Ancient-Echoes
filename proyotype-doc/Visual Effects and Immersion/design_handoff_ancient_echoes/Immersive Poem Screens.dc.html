1Why it reads dull — and the immersive rebuild

You're right, and the reference is closer to the poem feeling for a concrete reason. Your prototype paints scenery as flat gradients plus geometric SVG silhouettes — hand-drawn hills, a circle for the moon, dashed lines for water. That renders the plan of 《山水情》, not the painting. The reference gets immersion from four things the prototype is missing: (1) real ink-wash art as a full-bleed ground, (2) a palette that breathes into daylight / paper instead of forcing night-purple onto everything, (3) depth — drifting mist, one living motion, a moon that stays put, and (4) the poem as the hero floating in 留白, breathing in line by line. Below are three moments rebuilt with those layers. The art layer is a drop-in slot — drag your painted ink-wash onto any screen (or click to browse) and it becomes the ground; everything else already moves.
